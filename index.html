Add the Project board to Buzz

Opening Buzz… If your browser asks, choose Open Buzz. Buzz then shows the plugin to review: press Install plugin.

Open in Buzz

After it's installed, open Project board in the Buzz sidebar and press Connect to link your own board key (about a minute).

Didn't open? In Buzz go to Settings, Plugins, Manage plugins, Add, and paste https://github.com/sunnnsolar/buzz-project-board. Needs Buzz 1.0 on a Mac.
